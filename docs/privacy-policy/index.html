Quoriva Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quoriva handles information in the Quoriva mobile app.

Quoriva is an offline-first study planning, focus timer, and review journal app. The current release does not require an account, cloud sign-in, advertising profile, or analytics account.

For Google Play listing purposes, the app name Quoriva and the privacy contact below identify the product and the responsible support channel for privacy questions.

1. Information handled on your device

Quoriva stores information you create or choose inside the app, such as:

  • Study plans
  • Task lists
  • Goals and progress
  • Focus timer sessions
  • Weekly focus statistics
  • Review notes and reflection answers
  • Disturbance notes
  • Favorite study tips
  • App settings
  • Language preference
  • Theme preference
  • Privacy consent status

This information is used only to provide app functionality, including showing your plan, tracking your focus progress, preserving review notes, and restoring your preferences when you reopen the app.

This information is stored in the app private storage area on your device. In the current release, Quoriva does not automatically transmit this information to Quoriva servers or third-party servers.

2. Information we do not collect

Quoriva does not collect or transmit personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Calendar events
  • SMS or call logs
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Microphone input
  • Camera input
  • Biometric data
  • Financial information
  • Government identifiers
  • Health records
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers
  • Crash analytics
  • Diagnostic telemetry

Quoriva does not run ads, does not use advertising SDKs, does not sell personal or sensitive user data, and does not build cross-app or cross-device behavioral profiles.

3. Permissions and external links

The Android release does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, phone, nearby device, notification, or external storage permissions for core functionality.

Quoriva opens official support, privacy, and terms links only when you intentionally tap them. These links open outside the app in your browser.

When you open an external website, your browser and the website hosting provider may process ordinary technical connection information such as IP address and browser request metadata. Quoriva does not attach your study plans, notes, focus records, or app state to those link requests.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Quoriva does not share your local study data with third parties in the current release.

Quoriva does not transfer your local study data to service providers, analytics providers, advertising networks, data brokers, or social platforms.

If you choose to export or copy your own app data, that action is controlled by you. You are responsible for where you paste, save, or send exported information.

5. Retention and deletion

Quoriva keeps your local app data on your device until you edit it, reset it, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the app data through your device controls.

You can review, edit, export, or reset local app data from within the app where those controls are available.

When you reset app data, Quoriva deletes the locally stored Quoriva state file and recreates a fresh local state. Deleted local data may not be recoverable.

Because the current release does not create online accounts, there is no remote account profile to delete. If account or cloud features are added later, Quoriva will provide account and associated data deletion instructions before those features are enabled.

6. Security

Quoriva limits data handling to local app functionality in the current release.

Local app data is stored in the app private storage area provided by the operating system. Protect your device lock screen and backups, especially if you write sensitive notes.

If future versions transmit personal or sensitive user data, Quoriva will update this policy and use modern cryptography such as HTTPS for data in transit.

7. Your choices and rights

You can use Quoriva without creating an account.

You can choose what study notes, tasks, plans, and review answers you enter.

You can review and edit your in-app content, reset local records, and contact us with privacy questions or requests.

If you share a device, manage exported data carefully and avoid storing sensitive notes in shared locations.

8. Children

Quoriva is designed for general study productivity and is not directed to children as its primary audience.

Quoriva does not knowingly collect personal information from children, does not show targeted advertising, and does not include child behavior tracking in the current release.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has entered sensitive information into local notes on a shared device, they can reset the local app data or contact us for guidance.

9. International users

Quoriva may be available to users in multiple regions. Because the current release stores app data locally and does not operate a remote account service, privacy requests usually relate to data stored on the user device.

You may contact us if you have questions about access, correction, deletion, or privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change.

Material changes will be reflected in the in-app Privacy Policy and at the policy URL before new data practices are enabled.
